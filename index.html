Sabse best ID kaunsi hoti hai?

Price akeli cheez nahi hai. ID kharidte waqt ye dekho:

  • Purane season ke items: Elite Pass, Booyah Pass aur limited bundles jo ab dobara nahi milte, inki value sabse zyada hoti hai.
  • Rare gun skins: Evolution (Evo) gun skins aur purane collab skins.
  • Characters: Alok, Chrono, K jaise popular characters unlocked ho.
  • Emotes aur bundles: rare emotes ID ko alag pehchaan dete hain.
  • Level aur rank: high level aur achhe rank ka record.
  • Account ki umar: jitni purani ID, utni zyada history.
  • Binding: ID kis account (Facebook, Google ya mobile) se juda hai, ye clear hona chahiye.

Jeetne ke tips

  • Zone ke andar pehle pahuncho, last moment pe bhaagna risky hota hai.
  • Fight ke baad turant cover lo aur health heal karo.
  • Squad ke saath callouts do: dushman ki direction aur distance.
  • Ek close-range aur ek long-range weapon rakho.
  • Training mode mein sensitivity set karke roz 10 minute practice karo.
